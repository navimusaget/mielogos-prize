Submission by Email

Email submission · Instructions

How to submit by email

Download the simple email-entry template (.txt) — fill it in, attach it with your final review copy, and send it to the official Prize address. You may alternatively copy the answers into your email body.

Use the official Prize address prize@mielogos.com and the subject line MIELOGOS Prize 2027 — Email Entry — [Work Title]. Send the details below in the message body or in a plain-text attachment, along with the review copy. Please do not email passports, home addresses, full prompt histories, or other unnecessary personal information.

Required entry information

  1. Contact: administrative contact name, reply email and role (author, co-author or authorized representative); confirm authority if acting as a representative.
  2. Credited authors: names or pen names in order, preferred public credits, and confirmation that each participating human author is at least 18 years old on the submission date.
  3. Work: title; one category (Fiction, Poetry, Literary Nonfiction, or Graphic, Hybrid & Experimental Literature); original language; English review-copy language. If translated, identify the translator and relevant permissions. Include relevant collaboration information when applicable.
  4. Public release: controlling publication date or year (within the applicable 2024–2026 qualifying period), publishing route or venue if applicable, and a verifiable reference such as a URL, ISBN or catalogue entry. Explain any materially revised edition if relevant.
  5. Symbiotic Authorship declaration: describe in good faith how the human–AI creative relationship materially affected the work (up to 8,000 characters). Do not send prompt counts or complete chat histories.
  6. Short synopsis: up to 2,400 characters.

Review copy and optional cover

Attach your complete final English review copy as EPUB or PDF; DOCX may be used where permitted. The Prize's review-file limit is 10 MB for Fiction, Poetry and Literary Nonfiction, and 50 MB for Graphic, Hybrid & Experimental Literature. A cover image is optional (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 2 MB).

Important for large files: the Prize's file limit is not a guarantee that email providers will accept attachments of that size. If your message cannot be delivered with the file attached, contact the Prize before the deadline to request an expressly authorized private transfer arrangement. Do not publish your manuscript through an unrestricted or publicly shared link. A request for another transfer arrangement is not itself a completed submission.

Required confirmations

Include an explicit affirmative statement addressing each of the following:

  • I confirm that I have sufficient rights and permissions to enter the work, and that the information I provide is accurate and offered in good faith.
  • I accept the 2027 Terms of Entry, acknowledge the Privacy Notice, authorize the disclosed AI-assisted processing used by the Prize, and understand that no particular AI provider, model or human-only processing route is guaranteed.
  • I confirm that the attached review copy is the complete, final version I intend to have judged.
  • I authorize the submitted synopsis to be used in Prize publicity and recognition materials if appropriate.
  • If I attach a cover, I confirm that I have authority to permit its use for Prize publicity and archive purposes.

After you send

Keep your sent message and attachments. Sending an email does not automatically mean it was successfully received or formally acknowledged. The Prize will check administrative completeness, identify the accepted review copy and issue a formal acknowledgement separately. That acknowledged review copy becomes the locked competition version.

If you already attempted the online form and its status is uncertain, say so in your email and provide any request or receipt ID shown. Please do not create parallel competitive submissions for the same work.

Deadline: 31 May 2027 at 23:59 UTC. The authorized submission must be successfully received by the deadline. Email delivery can be delayed; there is no advantage to waiting until the final minutes.

Before sending, read the Eligibility, Entrant Guide, Terms of Entry and Privacy Notice. Email submission is an authorized entry route only after MIELOGOS formally activates it during the applicable submission period.

Ready to send your entry?

Include the completed entry details and the final review copy. Verify that all attachments are included before sending to the official Prize mailbox.

Compose email entry

A technical receipt confirms only delivery of specified materials. Formal acknowledgement of the competition version follows administrative verification.
