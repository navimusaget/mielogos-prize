Before you submit

Please check before sending.

  • Correct work?
  • Correct file?
  • Complete file?
  • Final version?
  • Opens correctly?
  • Correct title and author credit?
Check again. We are not in a hurry. There is no advantage to submitting early. Once your submission is acknowledged, this is the version we will judge.

Ordinary review-copy formats

EPUB or PDF are the primary formats. DOCX is a fallback for ordinary text-based work where layout is not constitutive.

Ordinary file limit: 10 MB for Fiction, Poetry and Literary Nonfiction; 50 MB for Graphic, Hybrid & Experimental Literature.

No password protection, DRM, executable content, dangerous macros/scripts or special credentials for ordinary access.

You will also tell us

How the work entered public circulation, the category, credited authorship, the English review version where relevant, and a good-faith account of how human–AI interaction materially shaped the work.
