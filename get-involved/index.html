An open invitation

Get Involved

A literary prize grows through careful reading, constructive disagreement and people willing to help good work find its readers.

We welcome independent readers, critics, literary communities and potential collaborators. You do not need to agree with us about AI or authorship to begin a conversation.

Ways to contribute

We would like to hear from you.

01 · Literary reading

Independent and shadow readers

We invite expressions of interest from readers, editors, writers, critics and literary scholars who may offer a thoughtful literary perspective in a specifically authorized reading exercise.

Reasoned judgment matters more than prestige or agreement with our views on AI.

02 · Dialogue

Constructive criticism

Question our assumptions. Identify weak arguments, overlooked risks, methodological problems or unfairness. Substantive disagreement is welcome.

We are open to suggestions about governance, judging, accessibility and public materials.

03 · Outreach

Information and literary support

Journals, reviewers, newsletters, podcasts, libraries and literary communities may help introduce the Prize to new readers and writers.

Informational cooperation does not require endorsement of the Prize's philosophy.

04 · Cooperation

Partnerships and new ideas

We welcome cultural, research, educational, accessibility and institutional proposals, including ideas not captured by our existing plans.

You need not represent an organization or have a polished proposal to reach out.

Independence matters

Reading is not a vote.

Independent or shadow reading may add arguments and perspectives, but it does not automatically confer official judging authority.

Formal judges and any Reserve Human Judge are separately appointed under our rules. Learn more about judging and partnerships.

For prospective readers

Voluntary, unpaid. We are currently exploring unpaid voluntary participation, not offering employment or stipends.

Confidential. Manuscript access is not automatic. Assignments require separate authorization, confidentiality safeguards and limited access.

Independent. Personal, professional, creative and institutional conflicts must be disclosed and may require recusal.

Scope by agreement. Workload, timing and any credit will be discussed first. Contacting us does not guarantee an appointment.

No preferential treatment. Publicity, sponsorship and other support do not buy influence over eligibility, judging or recognition.

A short introduction is enough

Write in your own words.

No formal questionnaire. Briefly introduce yourself, mention your area of interest and what you would like to contribute or challenge. For reading, you can include preferred genres, experience, languages, examples of criticism or general availability. A CV is optional.

Please do not send ID documents, private manuscripts, confidential applicant material or complete AI conversations.
