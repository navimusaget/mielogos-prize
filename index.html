Literature first

We read the work. We ask about the relationship. We judge the literature.

Relation before percentage. Trust before surveillance.

MIELOGOS does not count AI-generated words, reward the number of models or demand complete prompt archives. A good-faith declaration is the normal starting point.

What Symbiotic Authorship means →

Judging

Openly human–AI. Institutionally human-responsible.

AI assessments are arguments, not votes. Disagreement is information, not failure.

How literary assessment works →

Plural assessment
One or more models, providers or analytical configurations may produce independent or comparative readings.
Progressively deeper human consideration
Works approaching Longlist, Shortlist and Winner receive progressively deeper human literary consideration.
Final responsibility remains human
No AI system exercises independent institutional authority over recognition.
