2027 Founding Cycle

Categories

Four entry paths, one literary standard. A work enters one category and follows one competitive path.

Fiction

Novels, novellas, short stories, story collections and other forms of fiction prose.

Poetry

A publicly released individual poem, sequence, chapbook or collection may constitute one entry. The object submitted must itself correspond to a publicly released literary object.

Literary Nonfiction

Including memoir, literary and personal essay, narrative nonfiction and related literary forms.

Graphic, Hybrid & Experimental Literature

For graphic narrative, hybrid and experimental literature whose form crosses conventional category boundaries or whose visual/material form is constitutive of the work.

One work, one path.

A submitted work enters one category and receives one Submission ID. Prize Administration may correct an obvious category mismatch before literary review where necessary.
