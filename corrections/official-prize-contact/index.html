Official Prize correspondence

Controlled correction of the operational correspondence channel for the MIELOGOS Literary Prize.

Effective 7 October 2026Scope: correspondence channel only
CurrentContactGoverning documentsPrize Rules / Founding Master

Correction

The designated official correspondence address for the MIELOGOS Literary Prize is:

prize@mielogos.com

Earlier locked Prize documents or historical references may preserve a previous Prize correspondence address, including prize@mielogos.org, or earlier framework references to the Association address contact@mielogos.org.

Those historical references remain preserved for documentary integrity. For current operational Prize correspondence, they are superseded by prize@mielogos.com.

Scope

This correction changes only the operational correspondence channel. It does not amend eligibility, submission dates, entry fees, literary assessment, recognition structure, contractual rights, privacy obligations, intellectual-property provisions or any other substantive Prize rule.

Association / Prize separation

General MIELOGOS Association correspondence remains separate from Prize correspondence. The Prize uses prize@mielogos.com for its official operational channel.

Document integrity

Locked documents are not silently rewritten merely to replace a historical email reference. Where relevant, the public HTML presentation carries this correction notice while the source document remains available in its locked form.
